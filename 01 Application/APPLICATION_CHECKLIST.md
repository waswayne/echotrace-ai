# Application Checklist

## Current Team Description

EchoTrace AI is a voice-first safety debrief assistant that helps workers turn
spoken recollections of workplace incidents and near misses into clear,
source-linked timelines.

It conducts a natural interview, captures important claims, identifies missing
details, flags possible discrepancies without judging truthfulness, and asks
neutral follow-up questions. The speaker can review, correct, and confirm the
final structured account before it is used in formal reporting.

The MVP is being built solo with AssemblyAI's real-time voice technology.
Feedback from workplace-safety professionals, field-operations teams, and
voice-AI builders would be valuable.

## Before Application Or Team Update

- [ ] Applicant and team details are accurate.
- [ ] Eligibility and team-size rules are verified.
- [ ] Product description matches the approved scope.
- [ ] No unsupported claims about accuracy, users, or partnerships appear.
- [ ] Responses meet all word or character limits.
- [ ] User explicitly approves the final text and external submission.
- [ ] Submission timestamp and confirmation are recorded.
