## Selected Direction

- Selected idea: EchoTrace Safety Debrief, pending final user confirmation.
- Target user: one worker recounting a workplace incident or near miss.
- Pain point: spoken recollections are natural but can be unstructured,
  incomplete, approximate, or corrected later.
- Why it could compete: it demonstrates voice-native narration and interruption
  while preserving provenance, uncertainty, and speaker control.
- Key assumption to test first: a neutral clarification loop feels helpful and
  accurate rather than accusatory or leading.
- Smallest credible demo: one fictional account becomes a source-linked timeline,
  one gap is clarified, one possible discrepancy is corrected, and the speaker
  confirms the result.

## Differentiation Warning

Generic voice incident reporting is already crowded. EchoTrace must demonstrate
the quality and integrity of a single speaker's correction process rather than
competing on transcription, work-order creation, or automatic reporting.
