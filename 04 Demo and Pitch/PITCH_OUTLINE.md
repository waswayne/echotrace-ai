# EchoTrace Demo And Pitch

## Three-Minute Story

### 0:00-0:20 - Problem

A worker may remember a near miss more naturally by speaking than by filling in
a form, but a recording alone leaves chronology, missing details, and later
corrections difficult to review.

### 0:20-0:35 - Product

EchoTrace conducts a neutral safety debrief and builds a source-linked account
the speaker can correct and confirm before formal reporting.

### 0:35-2:20 - Live Golden Path

1. Consent and non-emergency notice.
2. Open-ended fictional forklift near-miss narration.
3. Live transcript and source-linked claim cards.
4. One neutral question about a missing detail.
5. One possible time discrepancy shown with both source statements.
6. Spoken clarification and visible revision.
7. Barge-in during read-back to correct a detail.
8. Final timeline, unknowns, sources, and speaker confirmation.

### 2:20-2:45 - Technology And Responsibility

Show how AssemblyAI supplies the real-time voice session, turn handling,
interruptions, transcripts, and tool calls while EchoTrace supplies validated
state, provenance, neutral clarification, and revision history.

State the boundary: no truth score, no fault decision, and no automatic filing.

### 2:45-3:00 - Value

End with the reviewed account and the promise: spoken recall becomes a clear,
traceable draft without pretending AI can decide what is true.

## Demo Requirements

- [ ] Works from a clean browser session.
- [ ] Uses only fictional data.
- [ ] Voice is clearly essential to the demonstrated behavior.
- [ ] Source links and revisions are visible without explanation.
- [ ] The agent asks only neutral questions.
- [ ] Interruption occurs naturally and works reliably.
- [ ] The final account distinguishes confirmed and unresolved information.
- [ ] Timing remains below the verified event limit.
- [ ] Backup recording or screenshots are ready.
- [ ] Every product and technical claim is supportable.

## Presentation Guardrails

- Do not call EchoTrace a lie detector.
- Do not claim it prevents incidents or produces legally reliable testimony.
- Do not claim accuracy, latency, adoption, or cost savings without evidence.
- Describe commercial products and competing hackathon teams respectfully.
- Present limitations as deliberate safety and scope choices.
